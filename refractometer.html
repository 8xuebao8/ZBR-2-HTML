📏 折射仪Refractometer

利用“全反射临界角”测量宝石的折射率（RI），是最常用、最有说服力的鉴定仪器之一。

Measures refractive index (RI) using the critical angle of total internal reflection: one of the most useful identification instruments.

全反射与临界角Total internal reflection

光从高折射率介质（折射仪的玻璃棱镜，n≈1.86–1.89）射向低折射率介质（宝石）时，入射角大于“临界角”的光会被全部反射回来。临界角的大小只取决于两者折射率之比，所以只要找到明暗分界的位置，就知道宝石的折射率。

Light travelling from a high-RI glass prism (n ≈ 1.86–1.89) into a lower-RI gem is totally reflected beyond the critical angle. That angle depends only on the two RIs, so the position of the light/dark boundary (the “shadow edge”) gives the gem’s RI.

测量范围Range

常规折射仪量程约 1.35–1.81，上限受接触液（约 1.79–1.81）限制。钻石（2.417）等高折射率宝石会“超出量程（OTL）”，只能看到接触液的界线。

The usual range is about 1.35–1.81, limited by the contact liquid (≈1.79–1.81). High-RI stones such as diamond (2.417) are “over the limit” (OTL): you only see the liquid’s line.

光源Light

标准读数使用波长 589nm 的黄色单色光（钠光），阴影边清晰。白光下边界会变成彩色带，读数不准确。

Standard readings use 589 nm yellow (sodium) light, which gives a sharp edge. In white light the edge becomes a coloured band.

单折射与双折射Single vs double refraction

  • 单折射宝石：转动宝石始终只有一条阴影边。
  • Singly refractive: one shadow edge that never moves.
  • 一轴晶：两条边，其中一条固定不动（ω），另一条随转动移动（ε）。移动的边向高值方向移动 → 正光性(+)；向低值方向 → 负光性(−)。
  • Uniaxial: two edges; one stays still (ω), the other moves (ε). If the moving edge moves to higher values the stone is positive (+), to lower values negative (−).
  • 二轴晶：两条边都会移动。
  • Biaxial: both edges move.
  • 双折射率（DR）= 最大读数 − 最小读数。
  • Birefringence (DR) = highest reading − lowest reading.
  1. 用酒精清洁棱镜和宝石台面。Clean the prism and the gem’s table with alcohol.
  2. 在棱镜中央点一小滴接触液（不要太多）。Put a tiny drop of contact liquid in the middle of the prism.
  3. 把宝石最大、最平的刻面（台面）朝下，轻轻放在液滴上，推到棱镜中央。Place the stone table-down on the drop and slide it to the centre.
  4. 合上盖子，打开 589nm 光源。Close the cover and switch on the 589 nm light.
  5. 眼睛离目镜约 25–30cm 时可先找阴影边，再贴近目镜读数，读到小数点后第三位。Find the edge, then look closely and read to three decimal places.
  6. 把宝石每次转动约 30°，转满 180°，每次记录读数；必要时加偏光片分辨两条边。Turn the stone about 30° at a time through 180°, recording each time. Use the polarizing filter to separate two edges.
  7. 测完后取下宝石，立即擦净棱镜和宝石。Remove the stone and clean the prism and stone straight away.

点测法（远视法）Spot (distant vision) method

弧面型宝石（如翡翠）没有平的刻面，用极小的一滴液点接触。眼睛离目镜 25–30cm 上下移动，看液滴小像：一半亮一半暗时，对应的刻度就是读数（只读到小数点后两位）。

For cabochons (e.g. jadeite) use a tiny drop. Move your eye up and down 25–30 cm from the eyepiece and watch the image of the drop. When it is half light, half dark, read the scale (to two decimals).

⚠️ 接触液（含二碘甲烷）有毒，避免接触皮肤；棱镜玻璃较软（硬度约 5），不要在上面拖动硬物或划伤。⚠️ Contact liquid (diiodomethane) is toxic: avoid skin contact. The prism glass is soft (H ≈ 5): do not scratch it.
