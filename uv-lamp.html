在暗箱中用长波（LW 365nm）和短波（SW 254nm）紫外线照射宝石，观察它们发出的可见光——荧光。

In a dark viewing cabinet, shine long-wave (LW, 365 nm) and short-wave (SW, 254 nm) ultraviolet light on stones and watch for visible light they give off: fluorescence.

什么是荧光？What is fluorescence?

紫外线人眼看不见。某些宝石中的原子（如铬、某些缺陷或杂质）吸收紫外线的能量后，会放出能量较低的可见光，这就是荧光。关掉紫外灯后还持续发光的叫“磷光”。

We cannot see UV. Some atoms in a gem (chromium, certain defects or impurities) absorb UV energy and give it back as lower-energy visible light: fluorescence. A glow that continues after the lamp is switched off is phosphorescence.

长波与短波Long-wave and short-wave

  • 长波 LW 365nm：能量较低，荧光通常较强，大多数宝石用它观察。
  • LW 365 nm: lower energy; fluorescence is usually stronger and it is used most.
  • 短波 SW 254nm：能量较高，可帮助区分某些合成宝石（如合成红宝石、合成蓝宝石常有白垩状荧光）。
  • SW 254 nm: higher energy; helps detect some synthetics (synthetic ruby and sapphire often glow chalky).

铁会“熄灭”荧光Iron quenches fluorescence

含铁多的宝石（蓝宝石、铁铝榴石、橄榄石）通常没有荧光。所以泰国红宝石（含铁）荧光弱，缅甸红宝石荧光强。

Iron-rich stones (sapphire, almandine, peridot) are usually inert. That is why iron-bearing Thai rubies fluoresce weakly and Burmese rubies strongly.

“荧光 fluorescence” 一词就来自萤石（fluorite）。

The word “fluorescence” comes from fluorite.

  1. 在暗室中使用，让眼睛适应黑暗 1–2 分钟。Work in a dark room and let your eyes adapt for 1–2 minutes.
  2. 把宝石放在暗箱里的黑色无荧光底板上。Place the stone on a black, non-fluorescent tray inside the cabinet.
  3. 先开长波，观察荧光颜色和强弱（无、弱、中、强）。Switch on LW first; note the colour and strength (inert, weak, moderate, strong).
  4. 再换短波，比较两者有何不同。Then switch to SW and compare.
  5. 关灯后观察是否有磷光。Switch off and check for phosphorescence.
  6. 与已知标样对比，记录结果。Compare with known reference stones and record the result.
⚠️ 短波紫外线可伤害眼睛（角膜炎）和皮肤。务必通过暗箱观察窗观察，必要时戴防紫外眼镜。⚠️ SW UV can burn the eyes and skin. Always look through the cabinet window and wear UV-blocking glasses if needed.

荧光只能作为辅助证据：同一种宝石的荧光会因产地、杂质而不同。

Fluorescence is only supporting evidence: the same gem can vary by locality and impurities.
