🔬 宝石显微镜Gem Microscope

双目立体变焦显微镜，能放大 10–60 倍，是观察宝石内含物、判断天然与合成的最重要工具。

A binocular zoom microscope (10–60×), the key tool for studying inclusions and telling natural from synthetic.

Tip: focus on the table first, then slowly focus deeper. Inclusions sharpen at their own depth inside the stone.

为什么要放大观察？Why magnify?

宝石内部的“内含物”（包裹体）就像宝石的指纹，记录了它的生长环境。天然宝石、合成宝石和仿制品的内含物往往完全不同，所以放大观察是鉴定的第一步。

Inclusions are a gem’s fingerprints: they record how it grew. Natural, synthetic and imitation stones usually have very different inclusions, so magnification is the first step of any identification.

三种照明方式Three kinds of lighting

  • 暗域照明 Darkfield
    底座中心的挡板挡住直射光，只让光线从侧面斜射进宝石。背景是黑的，内含物把光散射出来而发亮，最容易发现。
    A baffle blocks direct light, so light enters the stone from the sides only. The background is black and inclusions scatter light and glow, so they are easy to spot.
  • 亮域照明 Brightfield
    光线从下方直接穿过宝石，背景明亮，内含物显示为暗色轮廓。适合观察色带、弧形生长纹。
    Light passes straight up through the stone; inclusions appear dark on a bright background. Good for colour zoning and curved striae.
  • 顶光 / 反射光 Overhead (reflected)
    从上方照射，看宝石表面：抛光痕、刻面棱是否锐利、表面坑点、拼合石的接合面等。
    Light from above shows the surface: polish marks, facet edges, pits and the join of composite stones.

放大倍数Magnification

总放大倍数 = 目镜倍数 × 物镜（变焦）倍数。一般先用低倍（10×）找到目标，再升高倍数细看。10 倍放大镜是钻石分级的标准倍数。

Total magnification = eyepiece × objective (zoom). Find features at low power (10×), then zoom in. A 10× loupe is the standard for diamond clarity grading.

  1. 清洁宝石，用宝石夹夹住宝石腰棱。Clean the stone and hold it by the girdle in the stone holder.
  2. 打开光源，选择暗域照明，关小光阑。Switch on the light, choose darkfield and close the iris a little.
  3. 从最低倍数开始，调焦看清宝石轮廓和台面。Start at the lowest power and focus on the outline and table.
  4. 慢慢向下调焦，一层一层扫描宝石内部。Focus down slowly, scanning the stone layer by layer.
  5. 发现内含物后提高倍数，重新调焦细看。When you find an inclusion, increase power and refocus.
  6. 换用亮域照明看色带，换用顶光看表面。Switch to brightfield for zoning, overhead light for the surface.
  7. 记录观察结果；关灯、取下宝石。Record what you see, switch off and remove the stone.
⚠️ 欧泊、珍珠等怕热宝石不要长时间放在光源上方。⚠️ Do not leave heat-sensitive stones (opal, pearl) over the light for long.
