⚖️ 静水力学天平Hydrostatic Balance

分别在空气中和水中称量宝石，利用阿基米德原理算出相对密度（SG）。不同宝石的相对密度差别明显，是很好的鉴定依据。

Weigh the stone in air and in water, then use Archimedes’ principle to work out its specific gravity (SG). SG varies a lot between gems, which makes it a useful identification test.

什么是相对密度（SG）？What is specific gravity?

相对密度 = 宝石的重量 ÷ 同体积水的重量。它没有单位。例如钻石 SG 3.52，表示钻石比同样大小的水重 3.52 倍。

SG = weight of the stone ÷ weight of an equal volume of water. It has no units: diamond’s SG of 3.52 means it is 3.52 times heavier than the same volume of water.

阿基米德原理Archimedes’ principle

物体浸入水中会受到向上的浮力，大小等于它排开的水的重量。所以“空气中重量 − 水中重量”就是同体积水的重量。

An object in water is pushed up by a force equal to the weight of water it displaces. So “weight in air − weight in water” equals the weight of an equal volume of water.

SG = A ÷ (A − W)
A = 空气中重量，W = 水中重量A = weight in air, W = weight in water

常见宝石相对密度Typical SG values

  1. 把天平放平稳，烧杯架跨在秤盘上方，不能碰到秤盘。Level the balance; the beaker bridge must straddle the pan without touching it.
  2. 烧杯装蒸馏水，吊篮完全浸在水中，然后归零。Fill the beaker with distilled water, immerse the basket fully, then tare.
  3. 把干净干燥的宝石放在上面的空气盘里，记下读数 A。Put the clean, dry stone on the upper (air) pan and note reading A.
  4. 把宝石移到水中的吊篮里，用针赶走表面的气泡，记下读数 W。Move it to the basket in the water, brush away any bubbles, and note reading W.
  5. 用公式 SG = A ÷ (A − W) 计算，保留两位小数。Calculate SG = A ÷ (A − W) to two decimals.
⚠️ 误差来源：气泡、水的表面张力、温度、宝石太小（小于 1ct 误差大）。多孔宝石（如欧泊、绿松石）不宜放入水中。⚠️ Sources of error: bubbles, surface tension, temperature, very small stones (under 1 ct). Do not immerse porous stones such as opal or turquoise.
