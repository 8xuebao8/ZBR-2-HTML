建议的鉴定顺序A suggested testing order

  1. 肉眼与放大观察：先看颜色、光泽、切工，再用显微镜看内含物。Look and magnify: colour, lustre and cut first, then inclusions under the microscope.
  2. 折射仪：测折射率和双折射率，这是最关键的数据。Refractometer: RI and birefringence, usually the most decisive data.
  3. 偏光镜：确认单折射、双折射还是集合体。Polariscope: confirm SR, DR or aggregate.
  4. 分光镜、滤色镜：了解致色元素。Spectroscope and Chelsea filter: find the colouring element.
  5. 紫外荧光灯：辅助判断，特别是合成与处理。UV lamp: supporting evidence, especially for synthetics and treatments.
  6. 静水称重：测相对密度，适合未镶嵌的宝石。Hydrostatic balance: SG, for loose stones only.

记住：任何一种仪器都不能单独下结论，要把几项结果结合起来判断。

Remember: no single instrument proves an identity. Combine the results.
