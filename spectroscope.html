🌈 分光镜Spectroscope

把白光分解成彩虹色的光谱。宝石吸收了哪些波长，光谱上就会出现对应的暗线或暗带，由此推断致色元素。

Splits white light into a rainbow. Wavelengths the gem absorbs show up as dark lines or bands, which point to the colouring element.

光谱是怎么来的？Where does the spectrum come from?

白光由红、橙、黄、绿、蓝、紫各种波长（约 400–700nm）的光组成。分光镜里的棱镜或光栅把它们按波长分开排成彩带。

White light contains every wavelength from about 400 nm (violet) to 700 nm (red). A prism or grating spreads them out into a band of colour.

为什么会有黑线？Why are there dark lines?

宝石中的致色元素会吸收特定波长的光，那些位置在光谱上就变暗了。我们看到的宝石颜色，是没被吸收的光混合出来的颜色。

Colouring elements absorb particular wavelengths, which go dark in the spectrum. The colour we see is the mixture of the light that is left.

常见致色元素的光谱Typical spectra by element

  • 铬 Cr：红区有锐利的细线（红宝石 694nm、祖母绿 683/680nm），黄绿区宽吸收带。
  • Chromium: sharp lines in the red (ruby 694 nm, emerald 683/680 nm) plus a broad yellow-green band.
  • 铁 Fe：蓝、蓝绿区的吸收带（蓝宝石 450nm、橄榄石 453/473/493nm、铁铝榴石 505/527/576nm）。
  • Iron: bands in the blue and blue-green (sapphire 450 nm, peridot 453/473/493 nm, almandine 505/527/576 nm).
  • 钴 Co：黄、绿、蓝区三条宽带，见于合成蓝色尖晶石和钴玻璃。
  • Cobalt: three broad bands in the yellow, green and blue: synthetic blue spinel and cobalt glass.

棱镜式与光栅式Prism vs diffraction grating

棱镜式：光谱明亮，但蓝紫区被拉宽、红区被压缩。光栅式：各波段间距均匀，便于读数，但亮度较低。

Prism: bright, but the blue end is stretched and the red end squeezed. Grating: evenly spaced and easy to read, but dimmer.

  1. 在暗环境中使用强白光（光纤冷光源最好，避免宝石发热）。Work in a darkened room with a strong white light (a cold fibre-optic light is best).
  2. 透明宝石用透射法：光从下方穿过宝石射入分光镜。Transparent stones: transmitted light, shining up through the stone into the spectroscope.
  3. 不透明或颜色深的宝石用反射法：光从侧面照到宝石表面，分光镜接收反射光。Opaque or dark stones: reflected light from the surface into the spectroscope.
  4. 调节狭缝：太窄太暗，太宽线条模糊。先开大找到光谱，再关窄看细线。Adjust the slit: too narrow is dark, too wide is blurred. Open it to find the spectrum, then close it for fine lines.
  5. 拉动镜筒调焦，使谱线清晰。Slide the draw tube to focus the lines.
  6. 从红端到紫端依次观察并记录吸收线位置。Scan from red to violet and note where the absorption is.
