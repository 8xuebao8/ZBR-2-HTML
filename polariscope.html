偏振光Polarized light

普通光在各个方向振动。经过偏光片后，只剩一个方向振动的光，叫“平面偏振光”。当上下两片偏光片的振动方向互相垂直（正交）时，光线完全通不过，视域是黑的。

Ordinary light vibrates in all directions. A polarizing filter passes only one direction: plane-polarized light. When the two filters are crossed (at 90°), no light gets through and the field is dark.

放进宝石会怎样？What does the stone do?

  • 单折射（均质体）：不改变光的振动方向 → 转动一周始终黑暗。
  • Singly refractive (isotropic): does not change the light → stays dark all the way round.
  • 双折射（非均质体）：把光分解成两束互相垂直振动的光，部分能通过上偏光片 → 转动 360° 出现四明四暗。
  • Doubly refractive (anisotropic): splits light into two rays vibrating at right angles, so some gets through → four light and four dark positions per turn.
  • 集合体：由无数取向不同的小晶体组成 → 始终明亮。
  • Aggregate: countless tiny crystals in all orientations → stays light.
  • 异常消光：单折射宝石内部有应力时，出现不规则的明暗斑块或“蛇形”带，但不会规则地四明四暗。
  • Anomalous extinction (ADR): strain in an isotropic stone gives patchy or snake-like light areas, never a regular four-fold pattern.

干涉图Interference figures

找到双折射宝石的光轴方向后，在宝石上方放一个玻璃干涉球（锥光镜），可以看到干涉图：一轴晶为黑十字加彩色圆环；二轴晶为一或两条弯曲的黑带；石英常见中心无十字的“牛眼”。

Looking down an optic axis with a glass conoscope sphere shows an interference figure: uniaxial stones give a black cross with coloured rings; biaxial stones give one or two curved brushes; quartz often gives a “bull’s-eye” with no cross in the centre.

  1. 打开光源，转动上偏光片至视域最暗（正交位置）。Switch on and turn the analyzer until the field is darkest (crossed).
  2. 把宝石放在载物台上，转动 360°，观察明暗变化。Place the stone on the stage and turn it 360°, watching for changes.
  3. 在宝石最亮的位置，把上偏光片转到平行位置，再次比较。At the brightest position, turn the analyzer parallel and compare.
  4. 换几个方向重复观察（宝石沿光轴方向时双折射宝石也会全暗）。Repeat in other directions (a DR stone looks dark when viewed along its optic axis).
  5. 需要时用干涉球寻找干涉图，判断一轴/二轴。If needed, use the conoscope to find an interference figure (uniaxial/biaxial).
