🔴 查尔斯滤色镜Chelsea Colour Filter

一片小小的双色滤光片，只让黄绿光和深红光通过。铬致色的宝石透过它会显出红色，是区分祖母绿与仿制品的快速工具。

A small filter that passes only yellow-green and deep red light. Chromium-coloured stones look red through it, so it is a quick check for emerald and its imitations.

查尔斯滤色镜由两种明胶滤片组成，只让约 570nm 附近的黄绿光和 690nm 以上的深红光通过。

The Chelsea filter combines two gelatin filters and lets through only yellow-green light around 570 nm and deep red light beyond about 690 nm.

为什么祖母绿会变红？Why does emerald turn red?

铬致色的祖母绿吸收黄绿光，却透过深红光。透过滤色镜时黄绿光已被宝石吸收，剩下的只有深红光，所以看起来是红色。而绿色玻璃、绿色电气石等仿制品透过的是黄绿光，所以仍然是绿色。

Chromium-coloured emerald absorbs yellow-green but transmits deep red. Through the filter the yellow-green is already gone, so only red remains and the stone looks red. Green glass or tourmaline transmit the yellow-green, so they stay green.

局限性Limitations

滤色镜只是辅助工具，不能单独下结论：有些祖母绿（含铁较多，如印度、赞比亚产）不变红，而合成祖母绿反应往往更强。染色翡翠也会变红。

It is only a helper, never proof on its own: some iron-rich emeralds (India, Zambia) do not turn red, synthetic emeralds often react more strongly, and dyed jade can turn red too.

  1. 用强白光（白炽灯或强光手电）从侧上方照亮宝石，背景用暗色。Light the stone strongly with white (incandescent) light against a dark background.
  2. 把滤色镜紧贴眼睛。Hold the filter close to your eye.
  3. 宝石放在离眼睛约 30–40cm 的地方观察颜色。Look at the stone from about 30–40 cm away.
  4. 把观察到的颜色与已知标样比较。Compare the colour with known reference stones.
